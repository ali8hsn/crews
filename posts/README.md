# Posts

Drafts for the eight-week content calendar (editorial dates 14 September – 8 November 2026).
They live here for review and move to the site when published; nothing here is live.

Weeks 1–2 are full drafts carried over unchanged apart from front matter. Weeks 3–8 are
being written from the calendar outlines.

Every post carries `title`, `description` and `keywords` front matter. Where a post makes a
claim about a competitor, the claim is sourced — see [docs/compare/](../docs/compare/) for
the underlying research and the date it was checked.
